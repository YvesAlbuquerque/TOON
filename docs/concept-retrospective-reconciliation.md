- **Game:** [T.O.O.N. — The Office Of Nonsense](../README.md), an independent historical living-studio management game, not an animation-authoring application.
- **Purpose:** explore the tension between the institutional *Office* and creative *Nonsense*, attachment to professionals and IP, and consequences visible over generations. [Player dream](design.md#player-dream) and [Soul Rule](design.md#soul-rule).
- **Project owner / final decision authority:** human project owner; artistic authorship, critical scope/finance/public claims, lifecycle gates and irreversible production/release decisions are not silently delegated to AI.
- **Sources:** [game design](design.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md), [Unreal AI strategy](unreal-ai-development-strategy.md), [coherence audit](preproduction-design-review.md); The Y Framework [lifecycle](https://github.com/YvesAlbuquerque/YFramework/blob/main/docs/framework/canonical-lifecycle.md), [gate contract](https://github.com/YvesAlbuquerque/YFramework/blob/main/docs/framework/integration-and-milestone-gates.md).
- **Technical direction:** Unreal **Accepted direction**; actual Unreal bootstrap **Planned**. Legacy Unity/Flux artifacts only historical migration evidence. [roadmap Phase 0.4](roadmap.md#04-unreal-bootstrap--planned).
- **Current bounded proof:** one complete 3–4-panel comic strip with 3–4 recurring professionals, two meaningful relationships, one tradeoff, explicit physical handoff/release, durable consequence and save/reopen. [First slice](design.md#first-validation-slice).
- **Non-goals:** full 1900–2030 campaign, all media shipped, eight finished archetypes, full local AI quality/capability claims, final game balance/commercial launch. [First-slice non-goals](design.md#explicit-non-goals-for-the-first-slice).
- **Resource ceilings:** **not decided**; owner must approve target machine(s), VRAM/RAM/storage/fps budget, acceptable latency, time and spend limit for each spike. Do not insert fabricated thresholds.
- **Stop/return:** suspend the relevant proof on a violated technical budget or disproven meaningful action; return to stages 04–08 only if evidence invalidates an authorial/player/product hypothesis, otherwise iterate stage 11–14 implementation.
- **Status:** draft charter for retrospective acceptance, not a claim that Stage 00 was run in the past.

## Selected Game Concept — indexed existing authority (Stage 04–09 synthesis)

**One sentence:** Manage an eccentric creative studio through changing media and generations, where the professionals and works you publish permanently change the office and the histories it leaves behind.

**Soul Rule (already accepted; not a newly invented rule):** **“Every production and every person can change the office that made them.”** [Source](design.md#soul-rule).

**Primary pillar:** *Doing* — managing creative chaos under uncertainty; intended emotional payoff is attachment and legacy. [Source](design.md#principal-pillar).

**Game loop:** recruit and manage recurring professionals; acquire/initiate production; assign people/time/costs; route the physical project through compatible stages; resolve human or creative tradeoffs; authorize release; observe reception and persistent changes; repeat as history accumulates. [Core loop](design.md#core-loop) · [physical workflow](design.md#production-as-a-physical-workflow-object).

**System spine / fragility (proposed risk synthesis):** 
- Staff relationships and capacity influence work, conflict, time and money;
- Workstations, player handoffs and version finality influence whether a production reaches release;
- Release/reception affect studio wealth, reputation, employee memories and IP/archive;
- Runtime AI supplies bounded content and possibly decisions but **does not own** authoritative economy, relationships, lineage or final release;
- Historical transitions must change decisions meaningfully without requiring every era in the first proof.

**Proposed project refusals for owner review (not yet owner-approved as a new Soul brief):** no animation DCC/player-built movie editor; no disposable generic workers as the sole people model; no automatic publication without the player; no replacing observable management consequences with isolated AI output; no requirement to deliver every media type or century in the first proof. Each is consistent with [design](design.md), but promotion to a formal refusals brief still needs owner acceptance.

**3Cs/interaction brief — to validate with prototype:** management camera looking across a small office, contextual selection of professionals/stations/project objects, physical project pick-up/handoff interaction, legible cost/choice feedback and pause/decision state. **Camera framing, exact controls, input device and interaction affordances are not specified by this sentence**; they require UX/technical tests and must not be treated as approved controls.

**First proof experience hypothesis:** a player can explain (without an investigator explaining the rules) how a relationship/crew problem affected a production and why their management choice left a persistent trace. This is proposed for validation, not observed behavior.

**Research update (2026-11-19):** [Audience and market snapshot](audience-market-snapshot-2026-11.md) has completed a bounded *secondary* survey of contemporary creative-studio/management games, observed Steam review signals, player-community discussions, platform requirements and a proposed 12–15-player qualitative study. This upgrades Stage 01 and Stage 08 evidence from *no dedicated market snapshot* to **Partially evidenced**, **not validated**: no T.O.O.N. prospective-player interviews, measured demand, acceptable hardware baseline, conversion/wishlist evidence or owner-approved commercial terms. Keep the Gate D decision pending.

## Audience and commercial evidence gap (Stage 01 + 08)

**Working hypothesis, explicitly unvalidated:** likely audiences include players who enjoy management simulation, creative-production games and emergent character stories. This is a *research targeting hypothesis*, not verified market sizing, willingness to pay or proven commercial fit.

**Required bounded research:** identify 3–5 appropriate contemporary comparables; examine documented player motivations, friction, typical sessions, performance expectations, product price/platform and launch scope; distinguish public sources from player observation; record confidence and dates. Use results to test the product promise and minimum viable scope. Do **not** infer that references such as *The Movies* prove market demand today.

**Open commercial risk:** the name T.O.O.N. was owner-selected despite known preliminary collision risk; clearance remains a separate specialized legal/commercial task before public commercial claims. [Project README](../README.md) · [YFramework route](https://github.com/YvesAlbuquerque/YFramework/blob/main/docs/ecosystem/independent-games/toon.md).

## Game Proposal — decision-facing retrospective draft (Stage 10)

**Opportunity:** a management simulation that makes the institution, its creative output and the people building it coevolve in visible, persistent ways.  
**Why this first proof:** the 3–4-panel strip is the cheapest bounded finished artifact that still connects staff, physical process, management pressure, published media and legacy; it does **not** validate later comics/animation.  
**Proof request:** authorize *planning and limited implementation only after applicable Gate D/E owner reviews*, beginning with bounded technical feasibility and a minimal deterministic management fixture.  
**Budget/staffing:** **not specified or approved**; owner/producer must set budget, responsible implementer, machine/performance target and stop-loss before authorization.  
**Primary threats:** unacceptable local image-generation cost/consistency; physically transporting stage artifacts feels tedious; relationships are opaque or ornamental; office scope overwhelms a small team; no credible target audience; commercial naming conflict.  
**Cut-order principle:** preserve meaningful people–production–consequence loop; reduce generated scope, visual breadth, historical coverage and special types before cutting the Soul-bearing player choice.  
**Decision sought:** owner reviews factual stage equivalences and authorizes a **bounded** retrospective Concept closure and subsequent Stage 11–12 specialist translation once risks, resource ceilings and thresholds are specified. **This document does not authorize it.**
