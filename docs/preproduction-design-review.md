**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

## Audit rule: do not ask to re-decide accepted design

A design gap exists only when the owning source lacks a necessary rule or contains incompatible rules. An **unimplemented** or **unvalidated** accepted rule is *engineering work or a validation gate*, **not** a fresh owner design question. Historical/legacy files and superseded PR text do not override the current design. Before escalating any question, search the current design, roadmap, relevant issues and merged PRs; record the conflicting passages and the consequential alternatives.

## Reconciliation performed in this PR

1. **Resolved documentation contradiction:** current high-level descriptions formerly implied text-only, illustration, animatics or realtime were additional production types. Align current README, design and roadmap to the **four** accepted types.
2. **Resolved initial-format omission:** record the owner's **accepted 3–4-panel comic strip first proof** in canonical design and roadmap without making Production comic-specific.
3. **Clarified automatic content versus physical stage progression:** a valid generated result can be the default selected version when the player does not intervene. **G-03 (owner, 2026-11-16) supersedes any implied automatic transfer between stations**: the player physically moves the production object to the next compatible workstation, advancing the chosen/default version and discarding the others. Final release remains separately player-authorized.
4. **Previous false positives withdrawn:** the original audit treated creative-participation budget, final release approval, reception/economy, local AI fallback and the ~40-hour campaign target as fresh owner decisions. They are already accepted design rules or validation work; **do not ask the owner to approve them again**.

## G-09 accepted — stylized country selection (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner selected **option A with a stylized presentation**: the campaign begins on a stylized world map/globe and the player chooses a **country**, not a city. **Owner correction (2026-11-16) supersedes the earlier broader interpretation**: the country is **only a source for studio/production language defaults and relevant historical/cultural events/context**. It does **not** drive wages, rent, talent, technologies, market opportunity, country bonuses, relocation, branches or multinational management. No mandatory city picker or geographic expansion system. Event facts need sourced content; the exact visual style and initial country set remain design/content work. This is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal validation.

## G-17 accepted — previous releases as continuation context (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **D** and clarified the intended mechanics: for a new production of an **existing studio-owned IP**, feed **the actual earlier published productions** to the local AI as ordered creative/continuity context so it can build the next installment. Do not add a mandatory end-of-release canon-review/approval screen. Reuse or develop earlier elements through the existing player-directed G-03 concept/script/art-direction surfaces. The game owns the published artifacts, provenance, identity constraints and the choice of safe, bounded context; the AI cannot silently rewrite shared IP identity or past works, and prior adaptation-specific details are not automatically universal canon. Unpublished drafts do not enter this published-work input; G-13 client-owned IP cannot be reused as studio continuations. Exact context retrieval/compression, long-history limits and conflict policy require validation; **Accepted direction / Documented**, not implemented or tested Unreal/local-AI integration.

## G-16 accepted — evolving IP continuity (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **option C — evolving continuity**. A studio-owned IP keeps a recognizable shared identity across works and media while allowing new details. Some are **persistent continuity changes** and others **specific to one production or adaptation**. Local AI generations provide candidate evidence, **never automatic authority** to rewrite shared canon: the next production uses previous published works as input under game-owned concept/continuity constraints, without separate post-release canon approval or per-detail player review. Preserve provenance, archived published outputs and parent/adaptation lineage, without retroactively rewriting released artifacts or reopening G-07 stages. Under G-13, commissioned client IP remains outside studio-controlled adaptation. **Exact promotion rules, conflict-resolution UX and weighting are still open design/validation**, not implemented Unreal behavior.

## G-15 accepted — global and production-type reputation (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **C**: the studio holds **one overall reputation** and **separate reputation for each of the four accepted finished production types**. Both the general standing and the relevant type-specific standing directly shape the **value and frequency of subsequent external offers**; success in comics gives some global credibility when entering animation but does not create full animation-specialist reputation. Delivery/release evidence modifies standing under game-owned rules; it does not introduce a client acceptance gate or quality-based withholding of the G-11 payment (G-12). Different production types remain available subject to other ordinary capabilities, not a permanent reputation lock. Cross-type weighting, update magnitudes, persistence, decay and offer-mapping need balancing and explicit game-owned proof. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal implementation.

## G-14 accepted — hybrid original-work revenue (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner chose **C**: a published studio-owned original can earn **initial commercial revenue at release** and **ongoing catalogue receipts** over simulation time. Both depend on game-owned reception, audience, medium, distribution and historical context; either may be zero, and catalogue income may change or end. Publishing is possible without separately negotiated distribution; specific paid marketing/infrastructure and income coefficients remain undecided. Holding a completed original unpublished generates neither income stream. Prevent duplicate launch/tick revenue across save/reload. Client-owned commissions under G-13 are excluded from studio-owned IP revenue; G-11 delivery payment remains independent. For qualifying employee-originated originals, the accepted **5% share of receipts from that production and its direct catalogue earnings** still applies. This is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not an Unreal implementation or balanced revenue model.

## G-13 accepted — commissioned IP belongs to client (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner selected **A**: a contracted client owns the IP created for its commission. The studio earns the G-11 delivery payment and its finished artifact/credits remain in the historical published catalogue, but this is **not studio IP ownership**, independent reuse rights, adaptation/sequel control or automatic recurring licensing/catalogue income. Do not offer a negotiable rights split or invent royalties. Studio originals and their existing originator-participation rules remain unaffected. **Owner clarification:** every external contract concerns IP that does not belong to the studio; no external offer may commission, license, reuse or transfer a studio-owned IP. Reject such an offer as invalid, not as a deferred rights-negotiation candidate. This is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not copyright advice or proof of Unreal implementation.

## G-12 accepted — final delivery; reputation shapes later offers (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner rejected the proposed client-approval/rejection mechanic: **once the work is delivered, it is delivered**. There is no client approval gate, post-delivery revision obligation, refusal to pay for perceived poor quality or quality/brief-compliance deduction from the guaranteed payment. **Artifact quality and brief adherence modify persistent studio reputation, directly feeding future external contract offer value and frequency.** AI evidence may inform a game-owned reputation model but cannot independently deny payment. G-11 pays on player-authorized delivery; already accepted explicit lateness penalties (G-05) remain separate and are not overridden by this quality rule. Reputation coefficients and future-offer mapping require balancing/game-owned validation. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not implemented Unreal behaviour.

## G-11 accepted — payment on external contract delivery (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner chose **option B**: signing a client contract pays **nothing upfront**; there are no contract advances or milestone instalments. The game settles the expected guaranteed payment only on **player-authorized release/contract delivery**, with no client veto or quality-based reduction; separately accepted explicit lateness penalties remain governed by G-05. A finished-but-unpublished work pays nothing and still faces its deadline. G-06 abandonment before release yields **no delivery income** and the existing global X% cancellation fee, with **no advance refund/offset case**. Verify exactly-once posting, persisted state and readable expected income versus actual cash. This is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not validated Unreal behavior.

## G-08 accepted — evolving physical publication; staffed transport deferred (owner, 2026-11-16)

Owner chose **alternative D**: start with a single physical publishing desk capable of releasing finished work; later allow optional specialized publishing-related facilities as the studio grows and eras evolve. Finished works can wait without automatic release. Player-controlled final release and the separate non-deletable catalogue remain authoritative, and contract deadlines/expenses continue while waiting. Additional archive/marketing/distribution structures, economics, effects and prerequisites are **not yet settled**.

**G-18 resolved (owner chose D on 2026-11-17):** future hired Production Assistants run **studio-wide automatic physical logistics** across all eligible projects, rather than needing per-move/project/station assignment. The manual move remains the fastest individual route; assistant worker-time/capacity and compatible station availability constrain logistics. Assistants may not make player creative/management choices, initiate creative rework, trash a project or approve publication, and must respect G-07 stage finality. The first vertical slice remains manual. G-19 resolves the transport-choice factors; override/pause, exception UI, hiring/unlocks and tuning remain unresolved. **Accepted direction / Documented; not implemented or validated.**

## G-19 accepted — three-factor Production Assistant job selection (owner, 2026-11-17)

When multiple production objects await transport, **each Production Assistant** chooses which to pick up using a **combined assessment of distance from the assistant to each waiting production, deadline urgency, and the assistant's relationship with the professional who last worked on that production** (not the next workstation's professional). Neither shorter deadline nor personal affinity overrides the other factors absolutely. Automated transport belongs to Production Assistants only; manual player carrying remains separate. G-20 resolves the strict external-before-original class priority; exact weighting and deterministic tie-breaking require balancing, while G-21 now resolves the choice of exclusive reservation for concurrency. This is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not a validated Unreal scheduler.

## G-20 accepted — contracts before originals in assistant transport (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **C**: Production Assistants select eligible external-contract transport jobs ahead of studio-original jobs. Only if no eligible contract project is ready for transport do assistants pick an original. Within the chosen class, the G-19 combined distance, urgency (where applicable) and relationship-to-last-worker rule still applies; deadline is not universally absolute inside the contract class. Original projects have no invented due date, aging boost or promotion ahead of waiting eligible contracts. A blocked/unavailable contract job cannot halt transport of otherwise eligible original jobs. This may starve originals during sustained contract backlog and needs playtesting, but the strict external-first preference is **Accepted direction / Documented**, not validated Unreal behaviour.

## G-21 accepted — automatic exclusive job reservation (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B**: each Production Assistant reserves an eligible physical project transport task before travelling to collect it. Other assistants exclude reserved projects and select a different eligible unreserved job according to G-20 (contracts before originals) and G-19 (distance, remaining deadline, affinity with the project's last worker). A reservation is exclusive but does not by itself advance a stage or authorize release. Atomic acquisition, one carried project per worker, save/reload and preventing duplicate handoffs are required engineering proofs, not validated runtime behaviour. **G-22 resolves automatic reservation release when transport is interrupted**, including where the physical object remains. G-23 resolves player takeover; exceptional invalid jobs still need implementation guards and validation. **Accepted direction / Documented.**

## G-22 accepted — interrupted assistant transport releases reservation (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B**: interruption of an automated transport releases its G-21 exclusive reservation. Before pickup the object stays at the original location; after pickup it remains at the physical point of interruption, so another assistant may reserve and collect it under G-19/G-20. Interruption alone does not cancel production, consume a creative version, re-charge creative costs, or commit the next G-07 stage. The object must remain reachable/recoverable across persistence boundaries, without stale reservation or duplicate handoff. **G-23 resolves manual takeover** of a reserved or in-transit object. Accepted direction / Documented, not runtime validation.

## G-23 accepted — immediate manual player takeover (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B**: the player can directly pick up an object already reserved or carried by a Production Assistant, without confirmation. Before assistant pickup, the assistant's reservation is canceled; during carriage, the same object transfers atomically from assistant to player and the assistant becomes available for other eligible work under G-19/G-20/G-21. No double carrier, duplicate handoff, creative version/cost side effect, silent stage commit or automated publication. Reachability, animation and state persistence remain implementation/validation tasks. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal validation.

## G-24 accepted — professionalization unlocks hiring Production Assistants (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner selected **C**. Hiring Production Assistants becomes an **optional** capability as the studio historically evolves toward more structured professional production practices. This is an organizational/progression milestone, **not a claim that the profession is a modern technological invention** and not approval for a specific calendar year, technology node, staffing count or number of manual productions. The early/first vertical-slice loop stays manually routed. After unlock, G-18–G-23 govern autonomous logistics and player authority. Trigger conditions, pacing, wages and presentation require subsequent balancing/validation. **Accepted direction / Documented**; no Unreal proof.

## G-25 accepted — Production Assistant logistics plus organization (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B**: Production Assistants physically transport projects and perform **operational production organization**: monitor/organize queues, track progress and notify the player about blockers. This is not broader creative, staffing or financial authority; they cannot decide new creative versions, bypass player decisions, change G-19/G-20 transport priorities, cancel projects or publish. The role stays optional and historically unlocked per G-24, with the first slice manual. Warning presentation, reporting frequency, performance and persistence are UX/engineering validation rather than further owner-approved autonomy. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal implementation proof.

## G-26 accepted — Marketing Department after strip-only period (owner, 2026-11-17)

The owner wants differentiated publicity/marketing options **similar in spirit to *The Movies***, available only after developing a **Marketing Department**. The department is **absent during the studio's comic-strip-only phase** and unlocks later in historical/media progression; the exact trigger remains undecided. A release still uses G-08's physical player-authorized publishing desk, with no mandatory marketing purchase. Promotional actions may affect reception/distribution but do not guarantee success or bypass G-14's game-owned revenue rules. G-27 now defines a modality with adjustable investment; exact modalities, spending balance, timing and historical constraints still need design/validation. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not runtime proof.

## G-27 accepted — modality with investment intensity (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **A**: after unlocking the G-26 Marketing Department, the player selects a **type/modality of promotion** and the **level/amount invested** in that campaign. More investment increases **potential audience reach**, not guaranteed success or profit. This does not require combining individually purchased publicity actions or delegating campaign strategy entirely to AI/staff. The department is absent in the strip-only phase and optional for publication. Exact modalities, numerical spending/reach model and repeatability remain open; G-28 resolves decision timing and cross-release effects. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal/runtime validation.

## G-28 accepted — marketing chosen before release, affecting both sides (owner, 2026-11-17)

The owner clarified the marketing campaign **decision is made before publication**, but the chosen G-27 modality and investment may influence **both pre-publication anticipation and post-publication reach/reception**. This is a single decision spanning the release boundary, **not** a new separate post-release promotional choice. Actual G-08 player-authorized release remains required; no original publication income or published-catalogue entry arises merely from marketing before release (G-14). Exact campaign lead time, effect duration and repeatability need further design/balancing; G-29 resolves abandoned promoted production consequences. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal implementation evidence.

## G-29 accepted — promotional sunk costs and reputation loss on cancellation (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner selected **B**: discarding an unreleased production with marketing forfeits its already spent promotional budget and may hurt reputation **in proportion to the actual audience anticipation created**. Low/no awareness can produce little/no penalty, while intensive effective publicity may worsen disappointment. Use G-15's global and relevant medium-specific reputation facets where appropriate. Existing G-06 fixed contract cancellation fee remains separate; do not double-charge or double-apply reputation effects across save/reload. No post-release income or cataloguing arises from a cancelled production; project-owned drafts remain permanently trashed. Exact penalty curves and public-expectation measurement require balance/validation. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal implementation proof.

## G-30 clarification — physical placement required for marketing decisions (owner, 2026-11-17)

The production's **physical object must be placed in the Marketing Department** before the player may choose G-27 marketing modality/spending or make any other future authorized promotional decision. No remote/global campaign decision UI. Committed G-28 pre-/post-release effects may continue after Marketing completes the job and returns the object to physical interaction. This is an **optional visit**, not an obligatory publication gate or reopening of G-07 closed creative stages; G-08 separate player-approved release persists. **G-30 owner chose A — a fixed campaign**: after confirming the modality and investment at the department, the promotional plan cannot be edited, supplemented, or repeated for that production, even if its object returns to marketing. Production delay does not reopen the decision; G-29 governs pre-release cancellation. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not implemented or validated.

## G-31 accepted — finished-but-unpublished marketing gate (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **A**: only a **fully completed, physically present, and still-unpublished** production may receive its one G-27/G-30 campaign decision in the Marketing Department. No concept-stage, mid-production or post-publication campaign creation. The player can bypass marketing and go directly to the G-08 physical publication desk; visiting marketing does not reopen G-07 creative stages, mutate final work or authorize release. Pre-release G-28 anticipation can arise after campaign choice, but post-release effects and G-14 publication income wait until player-approved publication; G-29 governs cancellation. The department is absent during the strip-only phase (G-26). **Accepted direction / Documented**, not validated Unreal gameplay.

## G-32 accepted — marketing is finite staff work over time (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B**: a G-27/G-30 fixed campaign takes simulation time to execute and consumes finite Marketing Department professional work capacity; it is not a full-effect instant purchase. **Owner correction (2026-11-17) supersedes the earlier release-while-working interpretation:** the G-31 completed/unpublished object must **remain physically occupied by Marketing throughout its execution**. During active processing the player cannot interact with, pick up, move, trash, or publish the production; on completion the physical object becomes available again for transport to G-08 player-authorized publication. Marketing workers have finite availability, but **owner technical correction (2026-11-17) serializes Marketing AI cover-art generation to one active job**; other jobs wait. When promotional art is generated, duration follows actual AI generation, not an artificial staffing timer. Effects across the publication boundary remain governed by G-28, cancelled works by G-29, and campaign parameters remain immutable per G-30. Campaign duration, workload allocation and interruptions remain balancing/engineering questions. An unfinished Marketing job cannot be published, so the proposed G-33 early-publication choice is not a valid design gap. **Accepted direction / Documented**, not Unreal runtime proof.

## G-33 accepted — gradual decay of anticipation while awaiting release (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner chose **B** for the revised valid G-33: **after Marketing has finished and released the physical object** under G-32, public expectation created before launch **decays gradually while the finished production remains unpublished**. Earlier G-33 early-publication-while-Marketing-still-works scenario remains invalid: active processing holds the object and forbids interaction. This decay is not an abrupt expiration date, a campaign refund, an automatic campaign cancellation or a blanket removal of G-28's potential post-release effects. G-08 still controls publication, G-30 campaign parameters remain fixed, and G-29 applies to abandoned promoted projects. Decay function, timing and market response are balancing questions; no Unreal runtime proof. **Accepted direction / Documented.**

## G-34 accepted — audience affinity modified by era and media type (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner specified a **combined rule**: promotional modalities have affinities for particular **audience segments**, and their **effects vary by historical period and by the production's media type**. Do not interpret this as one universal cost-efficiency ranking, a historical progression where newer is always better, or a rigid marketing subsystem independently hardcoded for each medium. G-26 historical availability, G-27 chosen modality/investment, G-30 fixed physical campaign, G-31 completed-project eligibility, G-32 staff-time/physical occupancy and G-33 anticipation decay remain binding. Audience taxonomy, specific modalities and era/media effectiveness matrices are content/balance work; no guaranteed G-14 commercial outcome or runtime validation. **Accepted direction / Documented.**

## G-35 accepted — professional estimates plus optional market research (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner combined **B + D**: the Marketing Department presents audience/modality recommendations with **uncertainty**, improved by experienced professionals; optionally, before the fixed G-27/G-30 campaign commitment, the player may request **market/audience research**, consuming staff time and resources and improving information without guaranteeing outcomes. Advice remains grounded in G-34 audience affinity, historical period and production media type. Research is separate from the irreversible campaign selection, never a mandatory publishing gate and never permission for staff/AI to change the campaign automatically. Physical G-31/G-32 rule applies while research actually processes the completed/unpublished production: the object remains occupied and unavailable until work finishes. Research scope, cost and reliability remain unresolved; G-36 accepts reusable but context-sensitive and aging knowledge across projects, without Unreal validation. **Accepted direction / Documented.**

## G-36 accepted — reusable, aging institutional marketing knowledge (owner, 2026-11-17)

Owner selected **C**: Marketing research knowledge persists at the **studio level** and may improve later audience/modality recommendations for relevant productions.
